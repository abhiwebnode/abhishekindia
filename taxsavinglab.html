TaxSavingLab

Trading taxes in India are a maze of slabs, set-offs and carry-forwards. TaxSavingLab reads your broker statement and works it out for you — for free.

Building FastAPI · Python · PWA
Visit TaxSavingLab ↗

Overview

Filing taxes as an active trader means juggling short-term and long-term capital gains, speculative and non-speculative income, set-offs and carry-forwards. Most people either overpay or guess.

TaxSavingLab takes the broker's own profit-and-loss statement, parses it, and computes the tax position — including slab derivation, rebates and loss set-off waterfalls — in a clean web app that works offline as a PWA.

What it does

  • Parses broker P&L statements directly (including Dhan's multi-sheet Tax P&L)
  • Handles CYLA / BFLA set-off and carry-forward logic
  • Automatic slab-rate tax derivation and 87A rebate handling
  • Itemised charges breakdown
  • Free to use, installable as a PWA
