Overview

CouponBoyz curates deals, discount codes and offers across popular Indian and global stores. It's a content-and-affiliate business that has been live and profitable for over a decade.

The current focus is automation — pulling deals from affiliate network feeds and automatically expiring dead listings, so the freshness stays high without the manual grind.

What it does

  • Curated deals and coupon codes across major stores
  • Over a decade of continuous operation
  • Affiliate-network feed integration (CSV / XML / API)
  • Move toward automated content and auto-expiry of dead listings
