About me

A one-person product studio.

I'm a solo founder and full-stack developer. No co-founders, no team — I handle product, engineering, design, and the boring business parts across every project I run.

My work spans consumer web and mobile: a hyperlocal discovery app, a set of free tools that fix everyday government-form headaches, a tax calculator for traders, and a decade-old deals site. On the side, I trade equities, F&O and commodities, and build my own TradingView indicators.

Some experiments become products. Some don't. I keep building either way.
