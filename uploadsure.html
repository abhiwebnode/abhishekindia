Overview

Anyone who has filled an SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NEET or passport form knows the pain: the upload validator rejects your file because it's a few KB too big or the wrong dimensions. UploadSure fixes exactly that.

It's a growing suite of single-purpose tools — compress to an exact KB target, resize to exact pixels, convert formats, and a full set of PDF utilities — all running entirely in your browser, so your documents never leave your device.

What it does

  • Compress images or PDFs to an exact KB target, with a live compression gauge
  • Resize to exact pixels — fill & crop, fit & pad, or stretch
  • 10-tool PDF kit: merge, split, rotate, reorder, watermark, sign, and more
  • Smart Compress for automatic one-tap shrinking
  • Application packs for SSC, UPSC, IBPS/SBI, JEE, NEET, PAN, bank KYC & passport photos
  • Everything runs client-side — files stay private
