Overview

NearPop answers a simple question: what's actually around me right now? Instead of generic city-wide listings, it uses your GPS position to surface nearby merchants, offers and services in Delhi NCR — the local layer the big platforms skip.

It ships as both a fast progressive web app and a native Android app, so it works whether someone taps a link or installs it from their home screen.

What it does

  • Live GPS-based discovery of nearby merchants and offers
  • Progressive web app + native Android build from one codebase
  • Merchant onboarding with a claim-and-verify flow for listing ownership
  • Push notifications for nearby deals
  • Built for the everyday Delhi NCR high street, not just big chains
